# 分镜 · 女娲造人与补天（纪录片版）

The second storyboard, as posted at the checkpoint and approved with "go as you plan". It shows two things the first
example doesn't: **questions about how to show the story** (the character's look, a violent source detail) and how
their answers became the build. The finished page was `02-女娲造人与补天-动画.html`; this folder has it without
the recordings, [02-女娲造人与补天-无录音.html](02-女娲造人与补天-无录音.html) (safe to Read and grep).

**Decisions the user approved** (reuse them as precedent):
- **Character look:** the source has Nüwa walk, bend down and shape people after her reflection, so she's a goddess in
  human form (long jade robe, red sash, flowing ribbons, hair in a bun), not the Han-relief snake tail.
- **Violence in the classics:** 《淮南子》 says she cut off the giant turtle's legs (断鳌足以立四极). The source text leaves
  that out, so the page does too: the turtle's legs turn into four stone pillars in a glow of light and the turtle fades
  peacefully into light. The chapter card that would have carried that line has no quote.

**文件** `02-女娲造人与补天-动画.html`
**观众** a 7-year-old, watching with a parent
**长度** about 5½ min: 45 narration lines, a title card, 7 chapter cards and an end card (source ~20% longer than Pangu).
Measured: 203 s of recorded speech.
**配音** same plan as Pangu (Chrome: embedded 云健 recording; Edge: live 云健; 女声 option; English live).

**比原文多加的内容** (everything else is story 02's `x`, word for word, full-width punctuation)
1. Prologue: 「在中国，有一个流传了两千多年的古老传说，讲的是一位女神怎样造人，又怎样补天。」 (Nüwa is named in 《天问》,
   about 300 BC, and 补天 is in 《淮南子》, 139 BC, so "两千多年" holds.)
2. Classical quotes on 6 of the 7 chapter cards (shown, not spoken).
3. Three lower-third facts: the character 人 (ch. 2), 五色 = 青赤黄白黑 (ch. 5), 天圆地方 (ch. 6).
4. End card: where the story first appears, plus 「下一集：神农尝百草」.
5. Visual-only beats: a ✓ checklist of what the world already has, with an empty "?" box that 人 fills later; a people
   counter that ends on 「数不清 · COUNTLESS」 (no invented number).

### 片头
**Title card** (7.2 s) 一个来自中国古代的传说 · A LEGEND FROM ANCIENT CHINA / 女娲造人与补天 / NÜWA MAKES PEOPLE AND MENDS THE SKY
- **画面:** dawn mist over a river valley (ink-wash), a faint five-coloured shimmer in the clouds · **音乐:** title

**1.** 在中国，有一个流传了两千多年的古老传说，讲的是一位女神怎样造人，又怎样补天。
In China, there is an ancient legend, told for more than two thousand years, about how a goddess made people, and how she mended the sky.
- **画面:** slow push-in · **Added**

### 第一章 · 缺了什么 · SOMETHING IS MISSING
**Card:** 「娲，古之神圣女，化万物者也。」 — Wa (as in Nüwa): a holy woman of ancient times, who transformed all things. — 许慎《说文解字》
- **画面:** spring day, green valley · **音乐:** new section *pastoral*

**2.** 天和地分开以后，世界上有了太阳和月亮，有了山和水，
After the sky and the earth came apart, the world had the sun and the moon, and it had mountains and water;
- **节拍:** on 「太阳」「月亮」「山」「水」 each line drawing appears with a ✓ (the sun rises, the moon appears, peaks glint, the river sparkles)

**3.** 慢慢地也长出了草和树，有了虫子、鸟和野兽。
slowly, grass and trees grew too, and there were insects, birds and wild animals.
- **节拍:** ✓ on 「草」「树」「虫子」「鸟」「野兽」; grass spreads from the river in time-lapse, trees sprout, butterflies, birds, deer

**4.** 可是，好像还缺一样东西。 — But it seemed that one thing was still missing.
- **节拍:** a dashed "?" box joins the checklist; the music thins to almost nothing

**5.** 有一位女神叫女娲。 — There was a goddess called Nüwa.
- **画面:** low angle (horizon lowered), rim light, ribbons · **节拍:** her name lights up

**6.** 她在大地上走着，走了很远很远。 — She walked across the land, on and on, very, very far.
- **画面:** tracking shot with parallax; a dotted trail behind her; morning → afternoon

**7.** 风吹着草，鸟在叫，可她总觉得太安静了。 — The wind blew through the grass and the birds sang, but to her it always felt far too quiet.
- **节拍:** gust on 「风」, birdsong on 「鸟」, the music drops on 「太安静」

**8.** 她走到一条河边，弯下腰—— — She came to a river, and bent down —
**9.** 水里有个影子，那是她自己。 — there was a reflection in the water. It was her.
- **画面:** close-up of the water; the reflection ripples, then settles

**10.** 女娲忽然明白了：缺的是像她这样会说话、会想事情的东西。 — Suddenly Nüwa understood: what was missing was someone like her, someone who could talk and think.
- **节拍:** the "?" box glows · **音乐:** magic

### 第二章 · 黄泥小人 · THE LITTLE CLAY PEOPLE
**Card:** 「天地开辟，未有人民，女娲抟黄土作人。」 — When heaven and earth first opened, there were no people yet. Nüwa shaped people out of yellow earth. — 应劭《风俗通义》 · **音乐:** egg

**11.** 她伸手到河边抓了一把黄泥，加上水，揉成软软的一团， — She reached down to the riverbank and scooped up a handful of yellow clay, added some water, and kneaded it into a soft lump,
**12.** 照着水里的影子捏起来。 — and began to shape it, just like her reflection in the water.
**13.** 捏出头，捏出身子，捏出两只手，两条腿。 — She shaped a head, she shaped a body, she shaped two arms and two legs.
- **节拍:** each part pops on with a pluck · **Label:** 黄泥 → 小泥人 / CLAY → LITTLE CLAY FIGURE
**14.** 她把小泥人放在地上。 — She set the little clay figure down on the ground. · **音乐:** impact (held breath)
**15.** 没想到，小泥人一落地就活了！ — And to her surprise, the moment it touched the ground, the little clay figure came alive!
- **节拍:** heartbeat, then a warm hit on 「活了」 → joy · **Label:** 小泥人 → 活了！ (shown on the word, not at cue start)
**16.** 它跳起来，又蹦又叫，围着女娲转。 — It jumped up, hopping and shouting, and ran round and round Nüwa.
**17.** 女娲高兴得笑了。 — Nüwa was so happy that she laughed.
**18.** 她给它起了个名字，叫做人。 — She gave it a name: she called it “person”, 人.
- **节拍:** 人 is brushed into the "?" box; lower third 「人 · RÉN — “人”字，像一个侧着身子站着的人。」 (**Added**)
**19.** 她接着捏，捏了一个又一个。 — She kept on shaping, making one after another. · counter 1, 2, 3…
**20.** 可是大地那么大，一个一个捏，要捏到什么时候呢？ — But the land was so big. Making them one by one — how long would that take? · pull-out, worry

### 第三章 · 满地是人 · PEOPLE EVERYWHERE
**Card:** 「乃引绳于泥中，举以为人。」 — So she dragged a cord through the mud and swung it high — and made people. — 《风俗通义》 · **音乐:** joy

**21.** 女娲想了个办法。 — Nüwa thought of a clever idea.
**22.** 她折下一根藤条，蘸上稀泥， — She broke off a long vine and dipped it in runny mud,
**23.** 朝着四面八方用力一甩—— — and swung it hard in every direction — (riser, whooshes)
**24.** 泥点子飞出去，落到地上，一个一个都变成了人。 — the drops of mud flew out and landed on the ground, and every single one turned into a person!
- **节拍:** drops arc out and pop into people; the counter races and ends on 「数不清」 · **Label:** 泥点子 → 人 · **音乐:** heroic
**25.** 就这样，大地上到处都有人了。 — And so, there were people all over the land.
**26.** 他们盖房子、种地、生孩子，日子过得热热闹闹。 — They built houses, farmed the fields and had children, and life was busy and cheerful.
**27.** 过了很久很久，出事了。 — A long, long time went by. And then, trouble came. · time-lapse, music drops out on 「出事了」

### 第四章 · 天破了 · THE SKY BREAKS
**Card:** 「火爁炎而不灭，水浩洋而不息。」 — Fires blazed and would not go out; floods spread far and would not stop. — 《淮南子·览冥训》 · **音乐:** tension

**28.** 有一天，天上轰的一声， — One day, up in the sky, there was a great BOOM — · silence, then the hit on 「轰」
**29.** 塌下来一个大洞。 — and a huge hole caved in.
**30.** 天一破，水就从洞里往下灌，像下了一场永远不停的暴雨。 — Once the sky was broken, water poured down through the hole, like a rainstorm that would never, ever stop. · build
**31.** 地也裂开了，裂缝里冒出大火。 — The ground split open too, and great fires leapt out of the cracks.
**32.** 洪水和大火一起来，房子没了，人们没有地方躲。 — The floods and the fires came together. The houses were gone, and people had nowhere to hide.
- **画面:** people huddle on a hilltop; no one is shown hurt · **节拍:** the house drawing is crossed out
**33.** 女娲听见了哭声。 — Nüwa heard the people crying. · close-up in the rain · **音乐:** sad

### 第五章 · 炼石补天 · MENDING THE SKY
**Card:** 「女娲炼五色石以补苍天。」 — Nüwa melted stones of five colours to mend the blue sky. — 《淮南子·览冥训》 · **音乐:** heroic

**34.** 她走遍各地，找来五种颜色的石头， — She travelled all over the land and gathered stones of five different colours,
- **节拍:** counter 1 → 5; lower third 「五色 — 古人说的“五色”，是青、赤、黄、白、黑。」 (**Added**)
**35.** 堆在一起用大火烧。 — piled them up together, and burned them in a great fire.
**36.** 烧了很多天，石头化成了一锅亮亮的浆。 — They burned for many, many days, until the stones melted into a whole pot of bright, glowing paste.
- **Label:** 五色石 → 亮亮的浆 · **音乐:** timelapse
**37.** 她端起这锅浆，一点一点补到天上那个洞里。 — She lifted up the pot of paste and, bit by bit, filled in the hole in the sky. · magic
**38.** 洞补好了。 — The hole was mended. · a breath of silence, then a full chord (grand)
**39.** 水不灌了，火也灭了。 — The water stopped pouring, and the fires went out. · water and fire drawings crossed out

### 第六章 · 四根天柱 · FOUR PILLARS
**Card:** no quote (see the decision above) · **音乐:** worry

**40.** 可女娲还是不放心：天会不会再塌下来？ — But Nüwa still felt worried: what if the sky fell down again?
**41.** 她找到一只很大很大的乌龟， — She found a turtle, a very, very big turtle, · divide
**42.** 用它的四条腿立在大地的四个角上，像四根柱子，把天顶住。 — and set its four legs at the four corners of the earth, like four pillars, holding up the sky.
- **画面:** a diagram of a round sky over a square earth; the pillars rise at the corners · counter 1 → 4
- lower third 「天圆地方 — 古人想象，天是圆的，地是方的。」 (**Added**) · **Label:** 乌龟的四条腿 → 四根柱子
**43.** 从那以后，天再也没有塌过。 — And from then on, the sky never fell down again. · grand

### 尾声 · 讲给孩子听 · A STORY FOR THE CHILDREN
**Card:** 「苍天补，四极正。」 — The blue sky was mended, and the four pillars of the world stood straight. — 《淮南子·览冥训》 · **音乐:** finale

**44.** 人们又开始盖房子、种地。 — The people began to build houses and farm the fields again.
**45.** 慢慢地，他们把这件事编成故事，讲给自己的孩子听。 — And little by little, they turned all this into a story, and told it to their children.
- **画面:** a family at a small fire; the parent points up; the camera tilts up to the stars · **音乐:** end

### 片尾
**End card:** 完 · THE END
- 女娲补天的故事最早见于西汉的《淮南子》，距今大约两千一百年；用黄土造人的故事见于东汉应劭的《风俗通义》，距今大约一千八百年。
- The mending of the sky was first written down in the Huainanzi, in the Western Han dynasty, about 2,100 years ago. The story of people shaped from yellow earth comes from Ying Shao’s Fengsu Tongyi, in the Eastern Han, about 1,800 years ago.
