神农尝百草的故事，最早见于西汉陆贾的《新语》和刘安的《淮南子》，距今两千多年；“一日而遇七十毒”就出自《淮南子》。尝断肠草去世的结局，是后来的民间传说。 The story of Shennong tasting the hundred herbs was first written down in the Western Han dynasty, in Lu Jia’s Xinyu and Liu An’s Huainanzi, more than 2,000 years ago. “Seventy poisons in a single day” comes from the Huainanzi. The ending with the heartbreak grass is a later folk tale.
