### 片头
**Title card** (7.2 s)
- **Text:** 一个来自中国古代的传说 · A LEGEND FROM ANCIENT CHINA / 盘古开天辟地 / PANGU AND THE BIRTH OF HEAVEN AND EARTH
- **画面:** ink in water, a wide shot
- **音乐:** title (opening low hit, strings, choir, xiao theme)

**1.** 在中国，有一个流传了一千多年的古老传说，讲的是天和地是怎么来的。
In China, there is an ancient legend, told for more than a thousand years, about how the sky and the earth came to be.
- **画面:** chaos, a slow push-in
- **音乐:** title
- **Added:** this line is not in the source

### 第一章 · 混沌 · CHAOS
**Card:**
- **Quote:** 「天地浑沌如鸡子，盘古生其中。」
- **Translation:** Heaven and earth were a formless chaos, like a hen’s egg — and Pangu was born within it.
- **Source:** 《三五历纪》
- **音乐:** chaos (low strings, choir, whispers, distant booms)

**2.** 很久很久以前，没有天，也没有地。
Long, long ago, there was no sky, and there was no earth.
- **画面:** ink-in-water chaos in dusky purple-grey, thickening

**3.** 没有太阳，没有月亮，没有山，也没有水。
There was no sun, no moon, no mountains, and no water.
- **节拍:** on 「太阳」「月亮」「山」「水」, each word's line drawing appears and is crossed out (NO SUN / NO MOON / NO MOUNTAINS / NO WATER)

**4.** 整个世界黑乎乎的，什么都混在一起，像一个大鸡蛋。
The whole world was dark and murky, with everything mixed up together, like a giant egg.
- **节拍:** on 「像一个」, the chaos gathers into a giant egg

**5.** 这个大鸡蛋里面，睡着一个人，他叫盘古。
Inside this giant egg, someone was fast asleep. His name was Pangu.
- **画面:** inside the glowing amber egg, Pangu curled up asleep with "zzz"; a slow push-in
- **音乐:** egg (warm strings, heartbeat, slow guzheng)
- **节拍:** on 「盘古」, his name lights up

**6.** 盘古在里面睡了多久呢？一万八千年。
How long did Pangu sleep in there? Eighteen thousand years!
- **节拍:** on 「一万」, a counter runs up to 18,000 YEARS · 年 and the swirling speeds up

**7.** 他就那么蜷着身子，一直睡，一直睡。
He stayed curled up like that, sleeping and sleeping.
- **画面:** more "zzz"
- **节拍:** the counter fades

### 第二章 · 苏醒 · THE AWAKENING
**Card:** no quote
- **音乐:** awaken (strings, short bowed notes, a wandering xiao)

**8.** 有一天，他醒了。
Then one day, he woke up.
- **画面:** it goes dark; close-up on his head
- **节拍:** on 「醒」, his eyes open and glow in the dark

**9.** 他睁开眼睛，什么也看不见。
He opened his eyes, but he couldn’t see a thing.
- **画面:** his eyes dart around

**10.** 伸伸手，摸到的全是黏糊糊的东西。
He reached out his hand, and everything he touched was sticky and gooey.
- **画面:** he reaches out; the camera follows his hand
- **节拍:** on 「黏」, sticky blobs and a gooey sound

**11.** 他想站起来，头顶被挡住了；
He tried to stand up, but his head bumped into something;
- **画面:** he pushes upward
- **音乐:** tension (taiko pulse)
- **节拍:** on 「挡住」, the shell bulges where his head hits it

**12.** 想伸开腿，脚也被挡住了。
he tried to stretch out his legs, but his feet were blocked too.
- **画面:** he kicks
- **节拍:** on 「挡住」, the shell bulges at his feet

**13.** 盘古急了。
Pangu got really upset.
- **画面:** close-up of his furrowed face; he trembles
- **音乐:** build (taiko roll, tremolo strings)
- **节拍:** a growl

**14.** 他摸到身边有一把大斧头，抓起来，
He felt a great big axe beside him, and he grabbed it,
- **画面:** the axe appears in his hand
- **节拍:** on 「斧头」, a metallic "shing"

**15.** 朝着头顶上用力一劈——
and swung it over his head with all his might —
- **画面:** he raises the axe
- **节拍:** a sound that rises until the hit

**16.** 咔嚓！
CRACK!
- **画面:** the swing, a white flash, lens flare, camera shake; the crack appears (the palette turns white-gold)
- **音乐:** impact (the music drops out, then a deep hit)

**17.** 大鸡蛋裂开了一道缝。
The giant egg split open with a crack.
- **画面:** the crack spreads, light bursts out from behind him, the camera pulls back
- **节拍:** crackling

### 第三章 · 开天辟地 · THE GREAT DIVIDE
**Card:**
- **Quote:** 「阳清为天，阴浊为地。」
- **Translation:** The light and clear became heaven; the heavy and murky became earth.
- **Source:** 《三五历纪》
- **音乐:** divide (choir, bells, rising guzheng)

**18.** 轻的、干净的东西，从缝里慢慢往上飘，飘啊飘，变成了天。
The light, clear things slowly floated up through the crack — up, up, up — and became the sky.
- **画面:** blue sky above; the top half floats up and becomes a glowing ceiling of cloud; wide shot; he crouches, facing up

**19.** 重的、浑浊的东西，慢慢往下沉，沉啊沉，变成了地。
The heavy, muddy things slowly sank down — down, down, down — and became the earth.
- **画面:** the bottom half sinks into layers of rock; the egg and the axe fade away

**20.** 盘古看见光了，他很高兴。
Pangu could see light! He was so happy.
- **画面:** he stands up to full height and smiles
- **音乐:** joy

**21.** 可是他马上又担心起来：天和地刚刚分开，万一又合回去怎么办？
But then he began to worry: the sky and the earth had only just come apart. What if they closed up again?
- **画面:** sky and earth press in closer and shake; he looks worried
- **音乐:** worry (tremolo)

### 第四章 · 顶天立地 · HOLDING UP THE SKY
**Card:**
- **Quote:** 「天日高一丈，地日厚一丈，盘古日长一丈。」
- **Translation:** Each day the sky rose one zhang higher, the earth grew one zhang thicker, and Pangu grew one zhang taller.
- **Source:** 《三五历纪》
- **画面:** daylight
- **音乐:** heroic (brass, choir, taiko, xiao theme)

**22.** 于是他站在天和地的中间，用头顶着天，用脚踩着地，使劲往上撑。
So he stood between the sky and the earth, holding up the sky with his head and pushing down the earth with his feet, pushing up with all his strength.
- **画面:** he holds up the sky, feet planted on the earth
- **节拍:** a drum hit at 1 s and another on 「使劲」

**23.** 天每天升高一点，地每天变厚一点，盘古的身子也每天长高一点。
Every day, the sky rose a little higher, the earth grew a little thicker, and Pangu grew a little taller.
- **画面:** a day/night time-lapse: clouds race past, he grows in steps, the rock layers thicken
- **音乐:** timelapse
- **节拍:** lower third 「一丈 · ONE ZHANG — 一丈，大约有一层楼那么高 / One zhang is about as tall as a one-storey house.」 (**Added**)

**24.** 他就这样撑着，一天，一年，一百年，一千年，不知道撑了多少年。
He kept on holding it up — one day, one year, a hundred years, a thousand years — nobody knows how many years.
- **画面:** the camera pulls back
- **节拍:** the counter jumps on each word: 1 DAY → 1 YEAR → 100 YEARS → 1,000 YEARS

**25.** 天很高了，地很厚了，再也合不回去了。
Now the sky was very high, and the earth was very thick. They could never close up again.
- **画面:** the time-lapse slows; wide shot
- **音乐:** grand
- **节拍:** a big chord; the counter goes away

### 第五章 · 化生万物 · THE WORLD IS BORN
**Card:**
- **Quote:** 「左眼为日，右眼为月。」
- **Translation:** His left eye became the sun, and his right eye became the moon.
- **Source:** 《五运历年纪》
- **画面:** dusk, lit from behind
- **音乐:** sad (a lament on the xiao)

**26.** 可是盘古太累了。
But Pangu was so very tired.
- **画面:** he is exhausted, a silhouette against the dusk

**27.** 他慢慢倒下来，再也没有起来。
Slowly, he sank to the ground, and he never got up again.
- **画面:** he kneels, then lies down; it turns to twilight; dust rises as he lands; his eyes close

**28.** 他倒下以后，身上的东西一样一样变了。
After he fell, the parts of his body began to change, one by one.
- **画面:** a shimmer over his body
- **音乐:** magic (bells, guzheng arpeggios)

**29.** 左眼变成了太阳，
His left eye became the sun,
- **画面:** dawn; the sun rises
- **Label:** 左眼 → 太阳 / LEFT EYE → SUN

**30.** 右眼变成了月亮。
and his right eye became the moon.
- **画面:** the moon appears
- **Label:** 右眼 → 月亮 / RIGHT EYE → MOON

**31.** 头发和胡子，变成了天上的星星。
His hair and his beard became the stars in the sky.
- **画面:** his hair and beard fly up and turn into stars
- **Label:** 头发、胡子 → 星星

**32.** 他呼出的最后一口气，变成了风和云。
His very last breath became the wind and the clouds.
- **画面:** his breath turns into clouds and wind
- **Label:** 气息 → 风和云

**33.** 他的血变成江河，
His blood became the rivers,
- **画面:** rivers flow out
- **Label:** 血 → 江河

**34.** 他的肉变成田地，
his flesh became the fields,
- **画面:** fields appear
- **Label:** 肉 → 田地

**35.** 他的骨头变成石头，
his bones became the rocks,
- **画面:** rocks appear
- **Label:** 骨头 → 石头

**36.** 他的身子和四肢，变成了大地上高高的山。
and his body, his arms and his legs became the tall mountains across the land.
- **画面:** mountains rise where his body lay (misty, in a shan-shui style) and his body fades
- **节拍:** a rumble
- **Label:** 身体 → 高山

### 尾声 · 新的世界 · A NEW WORLD
**Card:**
- **Quote:** 「故天去地九万里。」
- **Translation:** And so heaven and earth stand ninety thousand li apart.
- **Source:** 《三五历纪》
- **音乐:** finale

**37.** 从那以后，世界上有了白天和黑夜，
From then on, the world had day and night,
- **画面:** the sun and moon wheel across the sky, from day into night

**38.** 有了山，有了水，有了风。
it had mountains, it had water, and it had wind.
- **节拍:** on 「山」 the mountains glint, on 「水」 the water sparkles, on 「风」 a gust blows

**39.** 只是还没有人。
But there were no people yet.
- **画面:** a slow pull-out as the wheel keeps turning, ending at sunset
- **音乐:** end
